Car Wallet — Vehicle Expense & Maintenance Tracker

The whole vehicle history, in one quiet place.

A privacy-first vehicle expense and maintenance tracker for iPhone and iPad. Keep fuel, mileage, service, repairs, documents and ownership costs together.

Back to AviLab

Track your vehicle

Car Wallet is built for the real cost of owning a car: fuel, mileage, maintenance, repairs, documents and everyday expenses — logged in a few taps, not scattered across Photos, Notes and a glove box.

Maintenance & service

Record service history, set intervals and see remaining distance before the next visit. Reminders stay on your timeline so maintenance does not depend on memory alone.

Fuel, mileage & expenses

Log fuel purchases and mileage, then review consumption and spend trends. Track washes, parking, repairs and other vehicle expenses alongside the fuel history.

Vehicle documents

Keep insurance, registration and related papers with the vehicle card — ready when you need them, instead of buried in a camera roll.

Ownership cost & analytics

See spend over time, fuel price history and a cost-of-ownership view so you know what the car actually costs — not only the last fill-up.

AI receipt & document scanning

Optional AI Scan can read text from a receipt or document you choose. Check every field before you save. Core tracking works without AI; scan credits are optional.

Privacy first

Your data stays on the device unless you turn on sync. There are no ads in Car Wallet. Optional cloud sync, Google Drive photo backup and AI Scan are features you choose — not defaults that sell your log.

Studio privacy: AviLab Privacy Policy. Product page: car-wallet-glow.vercel.app/privacy.

Pricing

Free to download. 30-day full trial. Lifetime unlock — a one-time price, no subscription and no ads. Extra vehicle slots and scan credits are optional purchases through Apple.

Frequently asked questions

What is Car Wallet?

Car Wallet is a vehicle expense and maintenance tracker for iPhone and iPad. It helps you keep fuel, mileage, service, repairs, documents and ownership costs together in one place.

Can I track vehicle maintenance?

Yes. You can record maintenance and service history, set reminders and track remaining distance for upcoming service.

Can I track fuel and mileage?

Yes. Record fuel purchases, mileage and fuel consumption, then review history and trends in analytics.

Does Car Wallet track car expenses?

Yes. It tracks fuel, maintenance, repairs and other vehicle expenses, and shows ownership-cost analytics.

Does Car Wallet require a subscription?

No. Car Wallet offers a one-time Lifetime Unlock. The app includes a 30-day full trial. There is no subscription in the current iOS products.

Does Car Wallet use AI?

AI scanning is optional. It can read receipts and documents you choose. Core vehicle tracking works without AI.

Which platforms are supported?

Car Wallet is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
