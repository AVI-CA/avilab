Sea Battle — Local Wireless Naval Game

Place the fleet. Find the ships. No chat required.

A classic naval battle for iPhone and iPad: versus the computer, nearby over local wireless, or online through Game Center. No ads and no AviLab account.

Back to AviLab

Three ways to play

Play versus the computer offline, host or join a nearby match over local wireless, or play online through Game Center — without an AviLab account or in-game chat.

Nearby over local wireless

Host or join a game on the same local network. Match traffic stays between the devices. Internet is not required for nearby play.

Online with Game Center

Play Online uses Apple Game Center. Call signs are short labels, not public profiles. Nearby play works without Game Center.

Quiet by design

No chat, no ads, and no AviLab login. Place the fleet, take turns, and finish the match.

Privacy

Versus-computer play is offline. Nearby play is local network only. Online play is handled by Apple Game Center.

Studio privacy: AviLab Privacy Policy.

Frequently asked questions

What is Sea Battle?

Sea Battle is a naval battle game for iPhone and iPad with versus-computer, nearby local-wireless, and Game Center online modes.

Do I need internet to play nearby?

No. Nearby host and join use the local network. Internet is not required for that mode.

Does Sea Battle have chat?

No. There is no in-game chat and no AviLab account.

Does online play need Game Center?

Yes. Play Online uses Apple Game Center. Nearby play does not.

Which platforms are supported?

Sea Battle is available for iPhone and iPad on the App Store.
