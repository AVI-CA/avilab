SUPKA — Paddling Companion for iPhone

Record the session. Keep the route. Share a pin if you choose.

A paddling companion for SUP, kayak and open water. Track distance, pace and GPS routes on iPhone, with Apple Health support. Apple Watch companion coming soon.

Back to AviLab

Record every paddling session

Start a SUP or kayak workout on iPhone and keep distance, duration, pace and the GPS route in one logbook — not scattered across Photos and Notes.

GPS routes & maps

See the live track while you paddle, then review the full route after. Maps stay with the session so you can look back at where you went.

Apple Health

With your permission, SUPKA writes workout data to Apple Health so sessions can appear in Health and Activity rings. You can revoke access anytime in iOS Settings.

Liveguard & SOS share

Optional safety tools open Messages or the system share sheet with a Maps pin to a contact you typed yourself. They do not call 911 or stream live location to AviLab servers.

Logbook & stats

Browse weekly and monthly distance, share a session card, and keep a quiet history of your time on the water.

Apple Watch — coming soon

The App Store release is for iPhone today. An Apple Watch companion is planned for an upcoming build.

Privacy

Workouts and GPS tracks stay on your device by default. Cloud features are optional. There are no ads in SUPKA.

Frequently asked questions

What is SUPKA?

SUPKA is a paddling companion for iPhone. It helps you record SUP and kayak sessions, keep GPS routes, and review distance and pace.

Does SUPKA work with Apple Health?

Yes. With your permission, SUPKA can read and write workout-related HealthKit data so sessions appear in Health and Activity rings.

Does SUPKA support Apple Watch?

Not in the current App Store build. Apple Watch companion support is planned for an upcoming release.

Does Liveguard call emergency services?

No. Liveguard and SOS only open Messages or a share sheet so you can send a pin to a contact you typed. They do not call 911.

Which platforms are supported?

SUPKA is available for iPhone on the App Store.
