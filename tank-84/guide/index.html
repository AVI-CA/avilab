TANK ’84

Hangar field guide — rules, tanks, and power-ups.

Product page

TANK ’84 is an original landscape arcade for iPhone and iPad. You command a tank, hold the hangar, and keep the shield at the bottom of the field intact.

Eighty-four stages. Twenty enemies each. Up to four on the field at once (six in two-player).

Play stages 1–3 free. Unlock Full Game (one-time IAP) for all 84 stages and random maps. Modes stay free — no ads, no energy.

How to Play

Move

Steer with the pad. Destroy all twenty tanks before they crack the HQ shield.

Fire

The red button fires. Hold your lane and protect the hangar.

HQ Shield

The base at the bottom of the field. If it falls, the sortie is over.

Wood

Breaks from any shot. Soft cover for you — and for them.

Metal

Needs a STAR — armor-piercing rounds punch through.

A Hit

First strips your oar (if swimming), then your stars, then a life.

Drops

Bonuses fall from flashing carriers — spawns 3, 6, 10, 14, and 17. The pickup on the dirt pulses so you can spot it.

Bullets

Opposing shots cancel in mid-air. Use that lane fight.

Pause / Next

Pause anytime. After STAGE CLEAR, NEXT keeps your score, lives, and stars.

1P / 2P

1P is one pad. 2P is two phones in the room (Nearby) or Game Center — one tank per device.

Tanks

Your Tank

Green armor. Pad to move, red button to fire. Hold the hangar and the HQ shield.

Gray Crawler

Slow basic threat. Easy to read, still dangerous in a pack.

Red Scout

Small and fast. Hard to pin — don’t let it flank the base.

Blue Gun

Mid speed, fires often. Respect the rate of fire.

Yellow Armor

Heavy hull. Takes four hits before it goes down.

Power-ups

Helmet

Ten seconds of steel nerves — you shrug off hits.

Timer

Freezes every enemy on the field for ten seconds.

Shovel

Rebuilds the wood around HQ.

Star

One: armor-piercing. Two: dual shot. Three: an extra life.

Bomb

Clears every enemy currently on the screen.

1-UP

Extra life. Stack it when the hangar gets loud.

Oar

Drive on water for twelve seconds. A hit ends the timer; if you are still in the canal you can crawl to shore — the aura lasts until dirt.

This is not a remake of anyone else’s cartridge. The maps, art, and rules were built for this game. The name is a salute — 1984 arcade heat, and a nod to a real machine from Kharkiv.
