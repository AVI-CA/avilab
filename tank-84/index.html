TANK '84 — Retro Tank Battle for iPhone

CRT glow. Top-down tanks. Built for landscape.

A retro arcade tank battle for iPhone: versus the computer, two players on one device, or nearby over local wireless. No ads and no account required.

Back to AviLab

One player, same device, or nearby

Fight the computer solo, share one phone for two players, or host and join nearby over local wireless with Multipeer Connectivity.

Built for landscape

TANK '84 is designed for iPhone in landscape: a top-down arena, D-pad and fire controls, and a CRT-inspired look.

Nearby local wireless

Host or join on the same local network. Internet is not required. Match traffic stays between the two devices.

Arcade, not a social feed

No ads, no AviLab account, and no chat. Score initials are short labels for the hi-score table — not public profiles.

Privacy

Versus-computer and same-device play stay on the phone. Nearby play uses the local network. Game Center is off in the current release.

Studio privacy: AviLab Privacy Policy. Product page: tank-84-legal/privacy.html.

Frequently asked questions

What is TANK '84?

TANK '84 is a retro top-down tank battle for iPhone, played in landscape, with solo, same-device and nearby multiplayer modes.

Can two people play on one iPhone?

Yes. Same Device puts two sets of controls on one phone.

Do I need internet for nearby play?

No. Nearby host and join use the local network. Internet is not required.

Does TANK '84 use Game Center?

Game Center is off in the current App Store release. Nearby play does not need it.

Which platforms are supported?

TANK '84 is available for iPhone on the App Store.
