Built for landscape

TANK '84 is designed for iPhone in landscape: a top-down arena, D-pad and fire controls, and a CRT-inspired look.

Nearby local wireless

Host or join on the same local network. Internet is not required. Match traffic stays between the two devices.

Game Center

Optional two-player matchmaking through Apple Game Center. Nearby play works without it. Game Center is Apple’s service — we do not run a game server.

Arcade, not a social feed

No ads, no AviLab account, and no chat. Score initials are short labels for the hi-score table — not public profiles.

Privacy

Versus-computer play stays on the phone. Nearby play uses the local network between two devices. Optional Game Center sign-in and matchmaking are processed by Apple.

Studio privacy: AviLab Privacy Policy. Product page: tank-84-legal/privacy.html.

Frequently asked questions

What is TANK '84?

TANK '84 is a retro top-down tank battle for iPhone, played in landscape, with solo, nearby, and Game Center multiplayer modes.

How do two players play?

Two physical devices: Nearby over local wireless (Host / Join), or optional matchmaking through Apple Game Center. One tank per phone.

Do I need internet for nearby play?

No. Nearby host and join use the local network. Internet is not required.

Does TANK '84 use Game Center?

Yes — optional for two-player matchmaking. Nearby play does not need Game Center. Apple processes Game Center identity under Apple’s privacy policy.

Which platforms are supported?

TANK '84 is available for iPhone on the App Store.
